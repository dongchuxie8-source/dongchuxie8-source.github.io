abstract: >-
  HSSR-LM is a bidirectional translation system between crystalline porous
  materials, including metal-organic frameworks and zeolites, and natural
  language. It introduces the Hierarchical Structure Sequence Representation to
  encode complex crystal data into model-readable sequences and uses a curated
  multi-granularity dataset of 8,000 samples.
